# Human usability protocol

Status: pending. The team should invite five to eight consenting people, preferably first-time users. Use anonymous labels P01, P02 and so on. Do not collect real names or contact data. Test checkout only in a disposable environment with a made-up name and email supplied by the moderator.

## Moderator

Introduce the fictional shop and ask the participant to think aloud. Read goals without navigation hints. Start timing on task presentation, note first errors and assistance, and stop when the goal is achieved or the participant abandons it. Ask about the confusion before explaining the interface. Assign two or three scenarios per person and vary the order.

## Participant task sheet

1. Find the cheapest cookie. Find a flavour you might like, then explain why the Passport picked it.
2. Explore a different flavour from the map, then compare three cookies.
3. Fill a box of four, replace one flavour and preview a gift note. Save the selection, reload and find it again.
4. Find a workshop and a related topic. Increase the text size.
5. Review the basket and complete a fictional order using the supplied test details.

Blockers prevent completion, major issues force assistance, and minor issues cause brief friction. Retest the same goal after a fix under comparable conditions. Report task-level counts and times once observations exist.

## Post-test questions

1. How easy was it to find and choose a cookie (1–5)?
2. What part of personalising a box was unclear?
3. What did you expect would happen to the gift note?
4. What would you change first?

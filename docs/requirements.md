# Requirements

Team 11 – Cookie Shop

Written by: Jonas (jonashey1), on behalf of Team 11.

These requirements describe what our Cookie Shop website must do. They are based on the two user tasks in our project brief: ordering cookies (transactional) and finding and comparing cookies (informational). They also cover accessibility, privacy and performance.

| Id | Requirement | Serves | Check | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | A customer can select one or more available cookies, choose the quantity of each and submit an order using made-up contact details. | Primary transactional task: customers need to order cookies. We will validate this need during usability testing before Hand-in 2. | On the live site, open the cookie assortment, select two different cookies, choose a quantity for each and submit an order with made-up details. The site must accept the order and show a confirmation that it was saved. | Partially verified on production, 23 Nov 2026: a fictional multi-cookie order was submitted and a success reference displayed. The exact R1 two-cookie acceptance scenario was not separately recorded. |
| R2 | A shop administrator can view every successfully submitted test order, including the selected cookies and quantities. | Primary transactional task: the shop needs accurate order information to organise preparation. We will verify this with test orders. | On the live site, submit a test order for two different cookies. Open /admin and find the order. Both selected cookies and their quantities must match the submitted order. | Partially verified on production, 23 Nov 2026: the submitted order and matching reference were visible in Payload /admin. Exact item quantities for the R2 scenario were not independently documented. |
| R3 | A customer can find the available cookies and understand the flavour, description and price of each product before ordering. | Informational task: customers need enough information to choose cookies. Inspired by the product presentation of Het Koekemannetje; we will validate this during user testing. | Open the cookie assortment on the live site. Every available cookie must have a name, description, flavour information and price that can be read without submitting an order. | Partially verified on production, 23 Nov 2026: six cookies with names, flavours, descriptions and euro prices were visible on the ordering page. Separate assortment-page review remains to be recorded. |
| R4 | A customer can compare at least two available cookies using their product information before deciding what to order. | Informational task: customers want to compare flavours and prices. We will check whether users can do this during usability testing. | On the live site, open the assortment and find two different cookies. Their names, descriptions and prices must be accessible so the user can compare them. | Not yet fully verified: the two-flavour comparison task has not been documented as an observed user task. |
| R5 | A customer can complete the main ordering task using only a keyboard, without needing a mouse. | Accessibility: customers who cannot use a mouse must still be able to order. We will test this manually. | On the live site, start on the homepage and use only Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter and Space to navigate to the assortment, select cookies and submit a test order. Every interactive element must be reachable and have a visible focus indicator. | Not yet independently verified with recorded steps and results: perform and document the keyboard-only acceptance test. |
| R6 | A customer can read product information and complete the ordering task on a narrow mobile screen without horizontal scrolling. | Accessibility and usability: mobile users need to browse and order comfortably. We will check this at a 390 px viewport. | Open the live website in browser developer tools at 390 px width. Visit the homepage, assortment and ordering pages. All important text and controls must remain visible and usable without horizontal page scrolling. | Not yet independently verified with recorded viewport, device and result: perform and document the 390 px acceptance test. |
| R7 | A customer can submit a test order without providing real personal information, and the website does not request payment details. | Privacy: the course requires fictitious test data and prohibits payments. We will verify the fields and ordering process manually. | Open the order form on the live site. Submit an order using a made-up name and e-mail address. The order must be accepted without payment information, and no payment step may appear. | Verified on production, 23 Nov 2026: a fictional name and email were accepted; no payment details or payment step were required. |
| R8 | A customer can open the homepage and cookie assortment without an excessive loading delay under normal network conditions. | Performance: customers need to browse the assortment without unnecessary waiting. We will measure loading times during testing. | Open the live homepage and assortment in a browser with normal network settings and an empty cache. Use browser developer tools to check that each page's main content becomes visible within 3 seconds. | Not yet verified: measure and record load timing with an empty cache and normal network conditions. |
